AUSPLAY Running Participation Rates by State and Age (2025)

Choropleth map

Darker shading indicates a higher share of the age group who run.

Key insights

  • Participation drops steadily across older age groups.
  • Victoria records the highest single youth rate at 31.9%.
  • Northern Territory peaks in the 25–34 group (31.3%).
  • Participation falls below 3% for seniors aged 65+.

Why It Matters: Community Health

The health challenges that active, connected communities help to address.

Long-Term Health Condition Prevalence by Australian State

Heat map

Darker cells show a higher prevalence of each condition in that state.

Key insights

  • Mental conditions are the most prevalent nationwide (26.1%).
  • Tasmania records the highest rates of multiple chronic conditions.
  • Cancer, COPD and kidney disease have the lowest prevalence.
  • Queensland reports the highest mental condition rate at 30.8%.
  • Northern Territory shows the lowest arthritis prevalence at 11.2%.

Types of Diseases in Australia (2018)

Tree map

Rectangle area shows the disease burden measured in DALYs.

Key insights

  • Cancer and neoplasms generated the highest group burden.
  • Cardiovascular diseases totalled over 550,000 DALYs.
  • Coronary heart disease remained the leading individual condition.
  • Back pain recorded a high individual burden of 222,357 DALYs.
  • Oral disorders were the smallest disease group.

Key Takeaways

Together, the eight views show a clear story: running is popular, seasonal and strongest among younger adults, while chronic disease still places a heavy burden on the community.

Participation peaks young

Running participation is highest from 18 to 34 and falls below 3% for people aged 65+, so there is room for more inclusive events.

Timing is seasonal

Charity runs surge around July and winter is the busiest season, with NSW and Victoria leading in volume.

Health need is real

Mental health conditions affect 26.1% of Australians and cardiovascular disease is a leading burden, making accessible community events valuable.
